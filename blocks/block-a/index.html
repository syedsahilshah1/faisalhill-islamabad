Faisal Hills Block A: Plot Prices, Possession and Plots for Sale

Block A is the largest established residential block in Faisal Hills, between Block B and the Executive Block, reached from the GT Road (N-5) entrance along the main boulevard. Roads are carpeted, utilities are in and families already live here, so buyers can build rather than wait.

It is also the society's most actively traded block. Developer inventory is reported to be exhausted, so almost every purchase here is a resale transfer rather than a fresh booking.

Where Block A Is

Block A sits between Block B and the Executive Block, off the society's main boulevard. Because it borders the Executive Block, the school, mosque and commercial area near the entrance are a short drive away.

Faisal Hills is marketed as an Islamabad address. The society itself lies in Rawalpindi District near Taxila, under the Rawalpindi Development Authority.

Files and possession plots

A file is a booking whose instalments may still be running. A possession plot has been handed over and can be built on. Possession plots trade at a premium over files in the same block and street. Block A listings usually state "possession", "NDC open" or "all dues clear", and those words move the price.

2 Kanal Plots, Commercials & Apartments in Block A

Signature Estates

2 Kanal Plots in Block A

Block A is one of only two blocks in the society offering 2 Kanal plots. At 75 × 120 ft they suit buyers building a large custom home rather than investors seeking a quick resale, and as the rate table shows, they are the best value per square foot in the block.

Commercial Boulevard

Commercial Plots in Block A

Commercial plots run from 9.6 Marla up to 2 Kanal. A boulevard-facing commercial plot of around 2.1 Kanal was recently listed at PKR 26 crore, close to PKR 27,500 per square foot, roughly four to five times the residential rate.

Sources disagree on whether the society's main commercial hub sits in Block A or in the Executive Block. Before buying commercial land here, confirm which commercial area you are buying into and what is planned around it.

Finished Units

Apartments in Block A

Two mixed-use buildings sit inside the block, which suits buyers who want a finished unit rather than land:

  • ZN Tower 1, in Block A Markaz, with one, two and three-bedroom apartments above ground-floor shops and offices. Reported construction progress ranges from 70% to 90% across sources, and the builder is described variously as ZN Builders and Gondal Group of Marketing.
  • Serene Hills, a mixed-use project by Makaan Solutions with apartments and shops.

Apartment pricing has been quoted from around PKR 13,000 per square foot, against roughly PKR 3,500 to 7,600 for residential land in the same block. You are paying for the built structure, so compare on total cost and rental potential rather than the headline rate.

Marketing Caution: One caution: some marketing for these towers describes Faisal Hills as a CDA-zone society and claims a five-minute drive to the airport. Neither is correct. The society is RDA-approved in Rawalpindi District, and airport drive times quoted elsewhere run to 40 minutes or more.

Buyer Evaluation

Who Block A Suits, and What to Weigh

It tends to suit families who want to build immediately in a finished block, buyers who want a larger plot at the society's best rate per square foot, and investors who value liquidity: Block A carries by far the deepest resale inventory in Faisal Hills, with roughly 400 plots listed on a single major portal in September 2026 against about 80 in the Executive Block.

Why Buyers Choose Block A

  • ✓Immediate Construction: Full ready possession with active utilities and 500+ resident families.
  • ✓Best Value on Larger Plots: 2 Kanal plots offer ~PKR 3,500/sq ft (half the per-sq-ft rate of 5M).
  • ✓Highest Resale Liquidity: Deepest market inventory in Faisal Hills (~400 active listings).

If you are buying as an investment, weigh these first:

  • !The price spread is wide: Asking prices for the same 5 Marla size ran from 55 to 95 lakh. Position, land condition and possession status explain most of it, so a plot is easy to overpay for without checking comparables.
  • !There is no developer payment plan here: With inventory reportedly sold out, you buy from a seller and settle in full, which rules out the instalment route available in newer blocks.
  • !Diligence sits on the transfer: The NDC, the possession letter and the allotment record matter more than anything else in the transaction.
  • !Larger plots are cheaper per square foot but slower to resell: 5 and 8 Marla plots have the deepest demand and highest liquidity.
  • !Published information about this block conflicts: Including where the commercial hub sits and where the Arc Monument is. Confirm anything that affects your plot's value.

We do not publish expected returns or appreciation figures for Block A, because no verifiable source supports them.

Resale & Legal Transfer Guide

How You Buy in Block A: Resale and Transfer

Because developer inventory is reported to be sold out, the transaction here is normally a transfer between a seller and you, completed at the developer's office on GT Road, Taxila.

Step 01

Agree terms with the seller

Agree terms with the seller, including who pays the transfer fee and any outstanding dues.

Step 02

Verify ownership at society office

Verify ownership at the society office: the name on the allotment letter must match the seller's CNIC.

Step 03

Check transfer history

Check the transfer history for repeated quick transfers.

Step 04

Obtain official NDC

Obtain the NDC (No Demand Certificate) confirming no dues remain.

Step 05

Ask for possession letter

Ask for the original possession letter where the plot has possession.

Step 06

Complete transfer at office

Complete the transfer with both parties present or properly represented, and pay only once it is recorded.

Step 07

Collect documents in your name

Collect the documents and updated allotment record in your name.

What you will need

  • • Copies of your CNIC, or NICOP for overseas buyers
  • • Copies of your nominee's CNIC
  • • Passport-size photographs
  • • The seller's documents and proof of payment
  • • Confirm the transfer fee and any NDC charge in writing before paying

Warning signs

  • • The seller's CNIC does not match the allotment letter
  • • The plot has changed hands repeatedly in a short period
  • • No NDC or possession letter can be produced
  • • The price sits well below market and no site visit is offered
  • • You are offered a file with no allotted plot number

Reading Block A Listings

Common terminology and acronyms decoded:

Series

The plot-number range within the block, such as the 3690, 3800 or 4210 series. Different series sit in different parts of the block.

MDR

Main double road. Wider, busier, and priced above internal street plots.

Sunface

A plot orientation buyers pay a premium for.

Margalla face

Frontage looking toward the Margalla Hills.

Solid land

Natural, level ground needing little preparation.

NDC open / all dues clear

The No Demand Certificate is available or dues are settled, so a transfer can proceed.

Possession and Building

Development means the infrastructure is finished: roads, sewerage, water, electricity and street lighting. Possession means the developer has formally handed your plot over and issued a possession letter, and only then can construction legally begin. In Block A, possession has been granted and plots are commonly advertised as ready to build. Confirm it in writing for your exact plot number, and check the society's construction approval requirements before you start.
