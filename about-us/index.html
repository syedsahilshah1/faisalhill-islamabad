Our Journey: From Strategic Vision to GT Road Landmark

2012

Establishment of Zedem International

Zedem International (Pvt) Ltd was founded with a singular conviction: to eliminate speculative real estate in Pakistan by securing land and complete legal NOC approvals prior to public launch.

2016

Strategic Site Acquisition on GT Road

Prime land was secured at the historic Margalla foothills along GT Road (N-5), strategically positioned 30 minutes from Islamabad Zero Point and directly linked to the M-1 Motorway.

2018

Official RDA Regulatory Approvals & Sanctions

Secured 100% verified legal NOC and layout approvals from the Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA). Ground breaking commenced with heavy earth-moving machinery, establishing the master road network and central boulevard.

2021

Rapid Infrastructure & Sector Handovers

Carpeted main boulevard, underground utilities, Grand Jamia Mosque, and primary family residential sectors launched with on-ground possession handovers in Block A & Executive Block.

2026

5,000+ Delivered Plots & Mega Skyscraper

Today, Faisal Hills stands as a fully operational landmark with over 5,000 satisfied investors, active construction of family villas, Hills Walk promenade, and the 27-storey Faisal Jewel skyscraper.
