Reading plot numbers

Listings often mention plot-number "series", such as the "800 series" in the Executive Block. These are ranges of plot numbers within a block. Always match a plot's block and number against the current approved map rather than an older download. Faisal Hills location guide explains how to reach the society from Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Terms You'll See in Faisal Hills Listings

Understand the standard market vocabulary before negotiating or signing an agreement.

File
A booking right to a plot not yet assigned a number or location; the final position depends on balloting.
Balloted Plot
A plot with an assigned number and confirmed physical location on the official map.
Possession
Physical handover of the plot by the society so architectural construction can immediately begin.
NDC (No Demand Certificate)
Official document confirming no financial dues or penalties are outstanding before plot transfer.
Solid Land / Cut Land
Listing shorthand for plots on natural ground versus plots formed by cutting into slopes (which may need more earthwork).
Series (e.g. 800 series)
A range of plot numbers grouped within a block designating a specific lane or sub-pocket.
Corner, Park-Facing, Boulevard-Facing: Positions that usually carry a higher market price or a 10% to 15% category premium charge at booking.

Check a Block Before You Commit

Follow this verified checklist before making down payments or signing transfer papers.

1

Confirm Plot & Block on Plan

Confirm the block and plot number on the current approved layout plan at the society office.
2

Verify Legal Authority Approval

Confirm scheme approval with the Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA).
3

Confirm Exact Plot Possession

Confirm possession for that exact plot number, not just the generalized block status.
4

Conduct On-Site Physical Check

Check the terrain and natural ground contour on-site, especially in hillside sectors like B Extension.
5

Confirm Sale Mode & Dues

Confirm the sale mode: full payment or installments, development charges, and any position premiums.
6

Confirm Transfer & NDC Papers

Confirm transfer requirements, including the NDC and official transfer fees in writing.
